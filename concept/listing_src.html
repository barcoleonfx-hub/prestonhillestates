About this home

Preston Hill Estates is pleased to present this extended, halls-adjoining semi-detached family home in one of Wembley's most settled residential streets. The house offers two generous reception rooms, four well-proportioned bedrooms and a ready-made additional room in the garage with ample space for a study or gym.

The property sits a short stroll from both Preston Road and Wembley Park stations on the Metropolitan and Jubilee lines, with local shops, popular schools including Preston Manor, and excellent transport links close at hand. Offered with no onward chain, it combines immediate comfort with immense scope to extend further, subject to the usual consents.
