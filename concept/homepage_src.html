The founder

The man behind the Hill

Every firm has someone who sets its tone. Here, that person is Jamil Ahmed. He opened Preston Hill Estates in 2004, already a seasoned hand after years in London property, and he has been at the branch nearly every working day since.

That constancy is why the firm still feels like it belongs to the neighbourhood. Markets have risen and fallen around it, but the way of doing business has not moved an inch. Jamil takes on the difficult sales, the awkward lets and the problems other agents hand back, and he stays with them until they are settled. Everyone who works here is held to the standard he set on the first morning: the client in front of you comes before anything else on your desk.

“Never back away from a challenge. Whatever it takes, every client leaves this office with a smile.”

Jamil Ahmed, Founder
